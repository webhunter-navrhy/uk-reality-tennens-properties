Description

This three bedroom semi-detached property located on the popular Moreton Hall is close to all local amenities and briefly comprises, entrance hallway, kitchen dining room, sitting room, three bedrooms, ensuite to master and family bathroom. Garden, driveway and garage. No Chain!

Entrance door into entrance hallway with storage cupboard and door to cloakroom / wc which is fitted with white suite of wc and wash hand basin. The kitchen is fitted with a modern range of wall and base units with integrated dishwasher, space for washing machine, space for fridge freezer, electric oven and gas hob with extractor over. The sitting room has patio doors overlooking and leading to the garden.

On the first floor the master bedroom has an ensuite fitted with shower cubicle, wc and wash hand basin with window to front aspect. There are a further two bedrooms and the family bathroom is fitted with bath and shower over, wc and wash hand basin and window to the front aspect.

Outside the garden is low maintenance with patio and shingle areas, the driveway is to the side of the property which leads to the garage.
